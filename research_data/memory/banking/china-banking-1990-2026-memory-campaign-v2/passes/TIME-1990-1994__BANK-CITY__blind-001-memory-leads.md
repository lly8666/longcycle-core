## Counterexamples / failed shortcuts

- “City commercial banks already existed as today’s institutions in 1990-1994” -> false/anachronistic shortcut.
- “Urban credit cooperative = rural credit cooperative” -> false shard-boundary shortcut.
- “Cooperative name means genuine member-governed cooperative” -> unverified and likely institution-specific.
- “Local government support is purely a credit positive” -> false; it can coexist with soft-budget and directed-lending pressure.
- “Local information advantage guarantees low credit losses” -> false; relationship pressure and correlated local shocks can dominate.
- “High local deposit share means strong solvency” -> false; funding stability and asset impairment are distinct.
- “Municipal merger is only scale expansion” -> false; it can be a loss-recognition, governance and resolution mechanism.
- “Later successful city bank history describes all predecessor cooperatives” -> survivor-bias risk.
