## Orthogonal challenge leads

### 1. The 2013 stress episode should be decomposed into structural reserve supply, autonomous factors and institution-specific demand
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Interbank stress can arise from the interaction of fiscal deposits, cash demand, FX flows, reserve-payment dates, maturing market positions and bank-specific settlement needs rather than one aggregate “liquidity number”.
- A future reconstruction should separate autonomous reserve drains/injections from discretionary PBOC operations and from distribution failures between banks.

### 2. Payment/settlement reserve balances and repo collateral availability are different constraints
- **Novelty:** new_category
- A bank may face a settlement-liquidity need even if it owns assets, while the ability to transform securities into cash depends on repo-market counterparties, collateral eligibility/haircuts and market confidence.
- This helps explain why aggregate system liquidity can coexist with acute funding stress at particular institutions.

### 3. SLF should be tested as a standing/backstop facility rather than ordinary scheduled OMO
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- The critical semantic is access to central-bank liquidity at a standing or backstop-style window with eligible collateral/institutions, not just another reverse repo.
- Its quoted rate may have corridor significance even if take-up is episodic.

### 4. MLF combines term funding and a central-bank price, but early usage should not be treated as the later dominant policy anchor
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Medium-term funding can relax banks’ liability constraints and provide a price reference at a longer tenor than ordinary OMO.
- The importance of the MLF rate in later LPR transmission is a later regime; historical records need a dated transition rather than backcasting it.

### 5. PSL is institution/channel specific and closer to targeted balance-sheet funding than generic market liquidity
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Its remembered connection to policy/development-bank financing implies a different transmission chain from commercial-bank MLF or system RRR cuts.
- A future ontology should represent recipient class/use channel separately from maturity and rate.

### 6. SLO/short-term special operations may represent an experimental instrument that did not become a durable framework pillar
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Preserve the possibility that some early liquidity tools were transitional or later deemphasized.
- Failure/dead-end history matters because a present-day instrument list can otherwise falsely imply all facilities coexisted with equal persistence.

### 7. First-generation LPR is a bank quotation/reference object, not necessarily an observed marginal transaction rate
- **Novelty:** new_category
- A quotation mechanism can provide a published lending reference while actual borrower pricing remains distributed around it according to credit risk, bank funding cost and commercial strategy.
- Therefore LPR, benchmark lending rate and actual weighted-average loan rate are distinct metric objects.

### 8. Benchmark rate, old LPR, interbank rates and central-bank facility rates formed overlapping price layers
- **Novelty:** new_category
- The operating framework was becoming more price-based without having a single clean transmission rate.
- Historical analysis should track which price affected retail deposits, bank loans, interbank funding and central-bank borrowing rather than force them into one “policy rate”.

### 9. Deposit-price liberalization changed the liability beta and could offset asset-side easing
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Greater deposit-pricing freedom can raise competition for stable funding, especially for weaker franchises, even while benchmark lending rates or broad policy settings are easing.
- Bank NIM/funding responses therefore become more heterogeneous after liability pricing liberalizes.

### 10. Targeted RRR cuts require an eligibility-state model, not merely a lower national reserve ratio
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- The research object includes which institution class or lending behavior qualifies, the duration/assessment of qualification and whether the benefit applies to all liabilities or a defined reserve basis.
- Exact criteria are intentionally not guessed.

### 11. Targeted relending/rediscount support may complement targeted RRR cuts for agriculture and small/micro borrowers
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Central-bank lending or rediscount facilities can direct cheaper/funded liquidity toward selected sectors without changing the system-wide reserve ratio.
- Their scale, pass-through and borrower eligibility are separate empirical questions.

### 12. FX intervention and reserve decline create a balance-sheet drain that broad easing tools can offset rather than “double ease”
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- When the central bank sells foreign assets/absorbs RMB in response to outflow pressure, RRR cuts or domestic liquidity facilities may partly replace lost base money.
- Gross easing operations therefore cannot be interpreted without the autonomous FX balance-sheet leg.

### 13. Excess-reserve remuneration is a plausible lower-bound candidate in an emerging rate corridor
- **Novelty:** new_category
- The rate paid on excess reserves can influence the opportunity cost of lending reserves overnight and therefore act as a floor-like reference under some conditions.
- Whether it was an effective lower bound in each subperiod is an empirical question, not assumed here.

### 14. SLF-type lending rates are plausible upper-bound candidates but access/frictions determine whether the ceiling is effective
- **Novelty:** new_category
- A standing central-bank borrowing option can cap market rates only if eligible institutions can access it, possess acceptable collateral and are willing to use the facility.
- Corridor theory and realized market ceilings should therefore be separate objects.

### 15. Repo-family rates became increasingly useful operational signals, but later DR007-centered practice should not be backcast wholesale
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Secured interbank repo rates may have become more central for observing liquidity conditions during this period.
- The later prominence of a particular depository-institution repo tenor/rate is a subsequent evolution; preserve date-vintage before using it as the historical operating target.

### 16. MPA broadens the controlled credit object beyond ordinary bank loans
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Memory suggests macroprudential assessment increasingly considered broader credit/asset expansion rather than only loan growth, alongside capital or other prudential dimensions.
- This matters because credit can migrate to bonds, interbank or off-balance-sheet channels when a narrow loan metric is constrained.
- Exact component definitions/weights require Evidence.

### 17. MPA is not the same institution as microprudential bank supervision
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Microprudential supervision focuses on institution safety/rules, while macroprudential calibration can use bank-specific constraints to manage system-wide leverage/credit conditions.
- The two may use overlapping metrics but should not share one historical state variable.

### 18. Local-government debt swaps/refinancing can change banks’ asset mix and liquidity profile without equivalent new credit impulse
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Moving a claim from a loan-like exposure into government bonds can affect duration, risk weight, collateral usability and reported loan growth.
- The monetary-transmission effect therefore depends on bank balance-sheet treatment, not just the nominal amount refinanced.

### 19. Interbank negotiable certificates of deposit (`同业存单`) became a material wholesale-funding channel
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Memory associates the mid-2010s with rapid development of NCD/interbank-CD funding, allowing banks—especially institutions with weaker deposit franchises—to raise market-based liabilities.
- This can increase sensitivity to market rates and support asset expansion even as traditional deposit competition intensifies.
- Exact launch date, issuer eligibility and regulatory treatment are Evidence work.

### 20. Targeted easing and broad easing cannot be inferred from the same headline instrument move
- **Novelty:** duplicate
- A targeted RRR cut or relending program can loosen selected channels while other banks/borrowers remain constrained; a broad RRR or benchmark-rate cut has a different eligible population.
- This restates the broad-pass targeted-versus-system-wide distinction.

### 21. The period still did not have one single modern policy-rate object
- **Novelty:** duplicate
- OMO rates, standing/term facility rates, benchmark rates, LPR quotations, Shibor and repo rates each carried different semantics.
- Treating one later-preferred market rate as the unique 2013–2016 policy rate would erase the transition being studied.

## Negative space / counterexamples

- System-wide reserve abundance does not rule out bank-specific settlement or collateral stress.
- A facility’s existence does not mean all banks could access it on identical terms.
- Old LPR quotation is not a transaction rate and is not the post-2019 LPR mechanism.
- A broad RRR cut that offsets FX-driven reserve drainage is not mechanically equivalent to the same cut under stable FX flows.
- Corridor candidates are not automatically binding corridor bounds; access and market segmentation matter.
- MPA and microprudential supervision are distinct even when they share bank-level metrics.
- NCD funding can loosen a bank’s liability constraint while increasing wholesale-market sensitivity.
- No material pre-1990 antecedent emerged.
