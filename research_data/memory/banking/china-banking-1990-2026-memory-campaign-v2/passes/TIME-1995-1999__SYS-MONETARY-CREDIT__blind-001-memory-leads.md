## Negative space / exclusions

- Do not equate cancellation of direct loan limits with complete credit-market liberalization.
- Do not treat M1/M2 announced targets, forecasts and realized growth as the same field.
- Do not backcast current reserve-account definitions or current RRR transmission mechanics into the late 1990s.
- Do not describe early open-market operations as today's seven-day reverse-repo/policy-corridor regime.
- Do not assume the early national interbank market used today's benchmark, participant set or settlement rules.
- Do not collapse Asian-crisis external risk, domestic deflation, bank NPL constraints and borrower demand weakness into one “credit crunch” label.
- Do not infer that an official rate cut or liquidity injection caused realized lending growth.
- Do not attribute infrastructure stimulus solely to monetary policy when fiscal bond issuance/project spending was an independent channel.
- Exact legal authorities, policy dates, RRR/rate values, money-supply target numbers, market launch dates and named operational rules remain deferred Evidence work after a future valid shard seal.
