## Orthogonal challenge leads

### 1. Social Financing is a financing-to-real-economy boundary, not simply “all credit”
- **Novelty:** new_category
- The emerging `社会融资规模` concept appears designed to measure financing obtained by the domestic real economy from the financial system, a boundary different from bank balance-sheet totals or M2.
- Financial-sector-to-financial-sector claims therefore should not automatically be treated as the same financing object.
- Historical reconstruction must recover the contemporaneous boundary and definition vintage rather than applying a present-day formula backward.

### 2. TSF flow, later stock measures and year-on-year growth are distinct vintages/objects
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Early policy use is remembered primarily as a period flow/aggregate-financing concept; later stock and growth series became more prominent.
- A single `TSF` field across decades would risk mixing flow, stock and revised-component semantics.
- Exact first stock publication and revision chronology require Evidence.

### 3. Entrusted loans do not represent ordinary bank balance-sheet credit creation
- **Novelty:** new_category
- In `委托贷款`, the bank can act mainly as an intermediary/agent for funds supplied by another party rather than lending its own deposit-created balance sheet.
- Counting it alongside RMB bank loans is useful for system financing but mechanically different for bank credit risk, liquidity and money creation.

### 4. Bankers’ acceptances need a funded-versus-contingent distinction
- **Novelty:** new_category
- An accepted commercial bill can represent a bank contingent/off-balance-sheet credit commitment before cash funding occurs.
- Once discounted, the financing can appear on a bank balance sheet in a different form; an “undiscounted acceptance” therefore should not be conflated with discounted bill lending.
- This is a high-value accounting/search semantic for TSF and credit-composition work.

### 5. Trust loans and bank-trust cooperation can transmit tightening outside standard loan statistics
- **Novelty:** new_category
- `信托贷款 / 银信合作` are remembered as routes through which financing could migrate when direct bank-loan pacing or balance-sheet constraints tightened.
- Regulatory treatment changed during the period, so the channel likely evolved rather than remaining one fixed workaround.
- Specific structures belong to the shadow-banking shard; here the lead is substitution in monetary transmission.

### 6. A bank’s internal annual credit budget is not identical to a PBOC legal loan quota
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Banks can allocate head-office credit budgets, branch limits and project approvals even without the old national loan-scale regime.
- Policy “窗口指导 / 均衡投放 / 把握信贷投放节奏” may influence those internal budgets without recreating the pre-1998 formal system.
- Evidence later should distinguish external guidance, supervisory pressure and internal bank allocation.

### 7. 2009 front-loading versus later “balanced pacing” is a timing-composition issue
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- A large share of annual loan creation may arrive early in a year when projects and banks rush approvals, creating different macro effects from evenly distributed annual lending.
- Annual totals alone cannot reconstruct the policy pulse; monthly/quarterly timing and loan maturity/composition matter.

### 8. Differentiated dynamic reserve adjustment is bank-specific and should not be stored as one uniform RRR series
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Memory suggests `差别准备金动态调整` introduced institution-specific reserve pressure based on some combination of credit expansion, capital strength/systemic role and macro conditions.
- It is neither simply the published uniform reserve ratio nor identical to a prudential capital requirement.
- The exact function/formula must stay unresolved until source verification.

### 9. The differentiated reserve mechanism is a monetary–macroprudential boundary object
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- It can absorb reserves and influence credit at the bank level while using prudential characteristics to calibrate intensity.
- Calling it the later `MPA` would be anachronistic; calling it “just RRR” would erase the bank-specific selection logic.

### 10. Policy/development banks had a distinct stimulus-transmission funding model
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Policy/development banks could support infrastructure or policy projects through bond-funded and policy-directed balance sheets that were not identical to deposit-funded commercial banks.
- Their response to reserve constraints, deposit competition and loan pricing could therefore differ materially.
- Exact institution/project shares are outside blind-memory confidence.

### 11. Large banks, joint-stock banks and local/rural institutions faced different reserve/deposit/capital constraints
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- The same aggregate RRR or benchmark-rate move did not produce the same marginal lending capacity everywhere.
- Deposit franchise, interbank access, capital headroom, local project demand and differentiated reserve treatment all plausibly created heterogeneous transmission.

### 12. Required reserves, excess reserves and reserve remuneration are three different liquidity objects
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Raising required reserves changes the compulsory reserve balance; a bank’s excess reserves determine its immediately usable buffer; remuneration affects the carrying cost/incentive of those balances.
- Historical “liquidity tightness” should not infer one from another without the appropriate metric.

### 13. Fiscal deposits could drain or release bank reserves independently of a change in the stated policy stance
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Tax collection, government deposits at the central bank and subsequent fiscal spending can create seasonal reserve withdrawals/injections.
- Quarter/year-end and Lunar-New-Year cash demand also move reserve conditions.
- OMO responses therefore may offset autonomous liquidity factors rather than signal a new macro stance.

### 14. FX-purchase liquidity likely changed character around the late part of the slice
- **Novelty:** new_category
- External-surplus/FX-purchase flows were still an important base-money source earlier, but memory suggests weaker or occasionally reversing FX inflows around 2011–2012 reduced automatic reserve injection.
- That would help explain a shift from mainly sterilizing inflows toward greater need for reserve cuts/reverse-repo style domestic liquidity support.
- Exact turning dates and sign changes require Evidence.

### 15. Central-bank bills, repo withdrawal and reverse-repo injection should be separated by direction and maturity
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- `央票`, repo and reverse repo cannot all be coded merely as “OMO”: they can withdraw or inject liquidity and have different maturity/rollover implications.
- The changing use of these instruments is potentially informative about the structural liquidity regime.

### 16. Shibor fixing versus actual unsecured trades versus repo transactions remains a critical market-rate distinction
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Shibor is remembered as a quotation/fixing framework from contributor banks, while pledged repo rates reflect secured transactions and actual unsecured interbank trades are another object.
- A rate spike can reveal marginal reserve pressure, but it is not automatically the central bank’s administered policy rate.

### 17. Negative real deposit rates can coexist with a nominal tightening cycle
- **Novelty:** new_category
- If inflation exceeds regulated deposit yields, household real deposit returns can be negative even while benchmark rates are being raised.
- That can encourage deposit migration toward wealth-management or other assets, changing bank funding and transmission.
- Therefore “rate hikes” do not mechanically mean household financial conditions were uniformly tight.

### 18. Targeted property/LGFV restrictions can tighten particular borrowers while aggregate money remains ample
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Property-purchase/mortgage/developer controls and later scrutiny of local-government financing can alter credit allocation independently of broad M2 or system liquidity.
- This is a counterexample to assigning one scalar “tight/easy” state to every borrower.

### 19. 2012 benchmark easing and wider bank pricing discretion are different policy dimensions
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Memory associates 2012 with both easier aggregate conditions and increased latitude around benchmark loan/deposit pricing.
- A benchmark cut changes the administered reference; wider pricing bands change banks’ ability to deviate from that reference.
- Those should be versioned independently in a historical pricing model.

### 20. “宽货币 / 宽信用” shorthand can obscure the difference between reserve liquidity and borrower credit creation
- **Novelty:** duplicate
- Reserve injection, M2 growth, bank loan creation and financing to specific real-economy borrowers are connected but not identical states.
- This reiterates the broad-pass heterogeneity/substitution warning rather than adding a new mechanism category.

## Negative space / counterexamples

- TSF is not simply a larger version of RMB loans; its boundary, components and vintage matter.
- An undiscounted acceptance is not automatically a funded loan, and an entrusted loan is not ordinary bank proprietary lending.
- Institution-specific differentiated reserves are not representable by one national RRR number.
- A Shibor fixing is not the same object as an actual repo transaction or a central-bank policy rate.
- A liquidity injection that offsets fiscal/cash/FX autonomous drains does not necessarily imply a macro easing decision.
- Tightening for property/LGFV borrowers can coexist with abundant system liquidity or easing for other sectors.
- The later MPA, mature TSF stock series and later shadow-banking taxonomy must not be backcast into this slice as contemporaneous labels.
- No material pre-1990 antecedent emerged.
