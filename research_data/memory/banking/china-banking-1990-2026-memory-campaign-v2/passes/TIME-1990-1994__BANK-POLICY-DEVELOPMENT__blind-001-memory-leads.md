2. **[N | high] The creation of dedicated policy/development banks around 1994 appears intended to remove or separate major policy-credit functions from state commercializing banks.** This is an institutional-boundary reform, not merely the founding of three new lenders.
3. **[N | high] Three distinct institutional mandates are recalled: a national development/infrastructure-oriented bank, an export-import policy bank, and an agricultural policy bank.** Their common `policy bank` label should not erase different assets, funding and counterparties.
4. **[N | high] China Development Bank / 国家开发银行 is recalled as the development/infrastructure member of the new architecture.** Exact founding mechanics and original mandate wording require Evidence.
5. **[N | high] Export-Import Bank of China / 中国进出口银行 is recalled as the foreign-trade/export-import policy-finance member.** Exact credit products and government-loan agency roles require Evidence.
