## A. Three policy-bank operating models diverge

1. **[N | high] By 1995-1999 the three dedicated policy/development banks should be modeled as materially different balance-sheet/business models, not one homogeneous `policy bank` type.** Development, export-import and agricultural policy finance differ in assets, funding and operating counterparties.
2. **[N | high] China Development Bank is recalled as concentrating on medium/long-term financing for national key projects, infrastructure, basic industries and pillar sectors.** Exact policy wording belongs to Evidence.
3. **[N | medium] `两基一支` is a useful possible historical search key for CDB's early project orientation.** The exact official scope and timing must be verified.
4. **[N | high] Export-Import Bank policy lending is recalled around export seller/buyer credit, equipment/technology trade and other state-supported external commercial activity.** The exact product set varied and should not be projected backward from later decades.
5. **[N | high] Agricultural Development Bank is recalled as tightly tied to grain/cotton/oil procurement, reserves and state agricultural circulation policy, with a liability model more dependent on policy/PBOC funding than ordinary deposits.**
6. **[R | high] The shared institutional purpose was to move policy-directed credit away from commercializing state banks, but practical separation remained incomplete.**

9. **[N | high] The creation/deepening of the interbank bond market in the late 1990s is a likely turning point in how policy-bank bonds were distributed and priced.** This is a MARKETS-BONDS bridge but directly affects policy-bank liabilities.
