## Safety net and resolution architecture

1. **[new_category][H] Explicit national deposit insurance becomes a new regime boundary around 2015.** This changes depositor-protection architecture even if no large bank is actually resolved in the slice.
2. **[useful_refinement][H] Deposit insurance ≠ guarantee that a failing bank will be liquidated.** It can coexist with takeover, merger, purchase-and-assumption, recapitalization or other interventions.
3. **[new_category][H] RMB 500,000 is a remembered insurance-limit figure.** Exact coverage unit, depositor aggregation and effective date are source-dependent and must not be promoted from memory.
4. **[useful_refinement][H] Coverage scope needs institution and claimant fields.** Domestic deposits, foreign-currency deposits, interbank deposits, bank senior claims and WMP claims may have different treatment; do not infer coverage from the word “deposit-like.”
5. **[new_category][M] Risk-based premium or differentiated premium is a possible design lead.** Exact premium structure and early implementation details need verification.
6. **[new_category][H] Deposit-insurance fund intervention powers are a resolution-interface lead.** Memory suggests the regime is not only ex-post reimbursement; it may support risk disposal under defined conditions, but exact tools and authority boundaries need evidence.
7. **[new_category][H] Safety-net design creates a new resolution clock.** Deposit-insurance coverage/compensation timing should be recorded separately from stress recognition, supervisory intervention, license change, legal liquidation and final claimant settlement.
8. **[useful_refinement][H] Protection of insured depositors does not imply protection of shareholders, subordinated creditors or uninsured claimants.
9. **[new_category][H] Deposit insurance may strengthen market-discipline rhetoric by making an explicit insured/uninsured boundary, even while actual bank exits remain rare in memory.
