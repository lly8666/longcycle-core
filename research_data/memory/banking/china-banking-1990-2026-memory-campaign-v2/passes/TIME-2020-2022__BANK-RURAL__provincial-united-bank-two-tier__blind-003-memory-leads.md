### A. Legal-person topology and successor mechanics

1. **[N | high] A provincial united-bank route can be a bank-form successor to the provincial-union platform while leaving county rural-commercial banks as separate legal persons.** The successor relation may therefore run from old provincial service/management entity to new provincial bank, not from every county bank into one merged provincial balance sheet.
2. **[R | high] County legal-person preservation is the decisive discriminator.** If county banks retain licenses, assets, deposits, capital and boards, the structure is two-tier even when provincial branding and management become much more unified.
3. **[N | high] Successor formation may transfer the provincial union's own assets, liabilities, staff, systems or service contracts without transferring county-bank loan books and deposits.** Replay needs separate predecessor edges for provincial-platform succession and county-bank continuity.
4. **[R | high] `联合银行` should be treated as a legal-form/topology clue, not evidence that all member banks are one consolidated bank.** Exact statutory meaning must be verified later.
5. **[R | high] Two-tier legal person and province-wide single legal person are mutually different perimeter models even if both are described publicly as provincial rural-credit reform.** The first preserves county banks; the second can turn predecessors into branches or otherwise eliminate separate balance sheets.
6. **[R | medium] Reform announcement, approval, legal establishment, business commencement, predecessor deregistration and systems migration can occur at different dates.** PIT replay should avoid assigning the final topology before the legally effective step.
7. **[N | high] The provincial entity may have its own banking balance sheet in addition to service-platform functions.** That creates a two-layer banking system rather than a pure non-bank association above county banks; exact permissible businesses and scale are Evidence questions.
8. **[R | high] A bank-form provincial entity can coexist with autonomous county lending franchises.** Provincial direct business, if any, should not be used to infer that county origination or customer assets have migrated.
9. **[R | medium] Common naming conventions can hide heterogeneous legal status across provinces.** `农商联合银行`, `农信联合社`, `农商银行` and similar labels should never be normalized by name alone.
