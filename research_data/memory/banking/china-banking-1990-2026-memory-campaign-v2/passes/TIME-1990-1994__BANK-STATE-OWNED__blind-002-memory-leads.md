## Orthogonal blind-memory leads

1. **[new_category | high] Policy-bank separation should be mapped function-by-function rather than bank-by-bank.** The development bank likely absorbed a portion of long-horizon infrastructure/industrial policy finance; the agricultural development bank took important agricultural policy-credit functions such as state procurement-related finance; the export-import policy bank took selected official export/import policy-credit functions. Commercial state banks retained adjacent commercial project, rural and trade-finance businesses.

2. **[useful_refinement | high] The carve-out therefore did not mean `CCB -> CDB`, `ABC -> ADBC`, or `BOC -> Exim` as whole-institution replacements.** The historical question is which portfolios, mandates, funding channels and staff/processes moved versus stayed.

3. **[new_category | high] Agricultural Bank and rural credit cooperatives may have had administrative/management links even though they must not be treated as the same legal/economic institution class.** Later evidence should distinguish ownership, balance-sheet consolidation, operational guidance and supervisory/agency relationships by vintage.

4. **[new_category | high] Period-native loan-product vocabulary may expose state-bank specialization better than modern sector labels.** Candidates include working-capital loans, fixed-asset/basic-construction loans, technical-transformation loans, agricultural procurement/policy loans and foreign-trade/FX credit. Exact product names and accounting boundaries need source verification.

5. **[new_category | high] Interest-accrual accounting is a possible bridge between weak asset quality and apparently positive reported earnings.** If unpaid interest could remain receivable/accrued for some period, accounting profit and cash recoverability could diverge materially. Historical rules and timing are uncertain and must be verified before factual use.

6. **[useful_refinement | high] `一逾两呆`-type old bad-loan vocabulary needs both stock and recognition-process semantics.** A loan might migrate from overdue to more severe categories only after time/administrative criteria, so old reported bad-loan numbers can lag economic impairment and cannot be translated directly into later five-category NPLs.

7. **[new_category | high] The later emphasis on `一级法人` is a useful governance search key precisely because early branch systems may have behaved more like semi-autonomous operating units than today's tightly centralized risk architecture.** The research question is the degree of branch credit authority, accounting responsibility and internal fund allocation by year/bank, not an assumption that branches were independent legal persons.

8. **[useful_refinement | high] Local-government influence may have operated through several channels: borrower/project sponsorship, pressure on branch lending/rollovers, personnel relationships and expectations of state support.** This is more specific than a generic `local influence` label and should be tested bank/region by bank/region.

9. **[new_category | medium] Internal funds-transfer arrangements across branches are a missing operating layer.** Nationwide deposit-rich branches could generate funds while policy-heavy/project-heavy branches consumed them; the bank's internal allocation mechanism may matter as much as each branch's local deposit base. Contemporary terms may differ from modern FTP.

10. **[new_category | high] Bank of China's balance sheet should be tested for dual-currency/foreign-exchange regime effects.** Foreign-currency deposits, trade settlement, exchange-control arrangements and overseas positions could create funding and asset risks not comparable to RMB-only domestic lending at ICBC/CCB/ABC.

11. **[new_category | medium] Professional-bank mandate boundaries were probably already eroding.** As commercial incentives increased, large banks may have competed outside their original sector niches, so a static four-bank division-of-labor chart would overstate specialization late in the slice.

12. **[new_category | high] Emerging `资产负债比例管理` / balance-sheet ratio management is a possible transition mechanism near the end of the slice.** It should be researched as an overlay that began constraining liquidity/capital/loan-deposit behavior while administrative credit planning still existed, not as an instantaneous replacement of the old regime.

13. **[new_category | medium] Enterprise settlement accounts and payments could reinforce lending relationships.** A state bank that held a major SOE's settlement/deposit accounts had information and transaction-flow advantages, but also could be pressured to keep credit flowing because payment, wage and working-capital continuity were intertwined.

14. **[new_category | high] Credit-risk mitigation standards were likely heterogeneous and often weak relative to later commercial-bank practice.** Administrative allocation, SOE status or government/project sponsorship could substitute for hard collateral/cash-flow underwriting in some lending, while other customers faced more conventional guarantees or collateral. Do not generalize this to all loans.

15. **[new_category | medium] Rollover and refinancing behavior deserves separate treatment from original loan approval.** A weak borrower might remain current in formal classification through renewals, extensions or new funding, making credit-flow continuity and reported delinquency two different objects. Exact practices are uncertain.

16. **[useful_refinement | medium] `企业化经营` reform should be decomposed into profit responsibility, cost control, branch incentives and bad-loan accountability rather than treated as one governance event.** Different elements may have moved at different speeds across banks and branches.

17. **[new_category | medium] Some policy-credit losses may have been treated as government/policy burdens rather than ordinary commercial credit losses.** Research should look for vocabulary around policy-loss accounts, fiscal compensation or designated write-off responsibility instead of assuming all credit losses sat on identical bank economics.

18. **[useful_refinement | high] The state-bank transition had a temporal boundary problem: formal commercial-bank legal architecture largely belongs after this slice, while late-slice reform policy already pointed toward commercial operation.** Evidence work must preserve the difference between announced direction, institutional setup and legally/operationally realized governance.

20. **[useful_refinement | medium] State ownership did not imply identical government objectives across all four banks.** Foreign trade/FX, agriculture, industrial-commercial working capital and construction/project finance created different ministry/sector/local-government interfaces and therefore different pathways for policy pressure and reform.

## Orthogonal counterexamples

- A branch can have material credit discretion without being a separate legal bank.
- A policy bank can absorb a mandate without absorbing all adjacent commercial products.
- ABC can have operational/administrative links with RCCs without RCC assets automatically belonging to ABC.
- Reported profit can rise while recoverability worsens if recognition/accrual rules differ; this is a hypothesis to verify, not a factual conclusion.
- Early balance-sheet ratio management can coexist with credit plans; `market-oriented` and `planned` are not mutually exclusive regime labels.
