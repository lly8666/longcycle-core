## B. Resolution vocabulary and state distinctions

17. **[new_category][high] `关闭` vocabulary.** `关闭` should be parsed as an administrative/operating action first; whether it also means immediate license revocation, liquidation or dissolution is case-specific.
18. **[new_category][high] `停业整顿` vocabulary.** `停业整顿` indicates suspension/rectification and can be reversible; it is not synonymous with bankruptcy.
19. **[duplicate][high] `接管` vocabulary.** `接管` is a formal supervisory-control state but can preserve the bank's license, legal person and customer operations during the takeover period.
20. **[new_category][high] `托管` vocabulary.** `托管` can refer to operational/management custody by another institution and should not be assumed to transfer ownership, liabilities or license.
21. **[new_category][high] `撤销` vocabulary.** `撤销` may refer to administrative withdrawal of an institution or license, but exact object—legal person, branch, permit or approval—must be read from the case document.
22. **[new_category][high] `解散` vocabulary.** `解散` is a corporate/legal-person state and can follow merger or voluntary reorganization without creditor impairment.
23. **[new_category][high] `破产` vocabulary.** `破产` should be reserved for a court/legal insolvency process, not used as a colloquial synonym for severe distress or regulatory takeover.
24. **[new_category][high] `清算` vocabulary.** Liquidation can be voluntary, merger-related, administrative or bankruptcy-related; the trigger and claimant waterfall matter.
25. **[useful_refinement][high] `吸收合并` is not bankruptcy.** Absorption merger ends one legal person through succession into another and can preserve customer claims in full.
26. **[useful_refinement][high] `资产负债承接` is not complete legal succession by default.** Distress transfer may move selected assets/liabilities while leaving a residual legal entity/estate; exact scope is a case fact.
27. **[new_category][high] `市场退出` is an umbrella term.** Policy documents may use market exit to aggregate merger, closure, revocation, branch conversion or liquidation; it cannot serve as the terminal-state label without decomposition.
28. **[useful_refinement][high] Operating-stop vs license-stop.** A bank may stop ordinary business while the license remains temporarily active for resolution, or lose a license while liquidation continues legally.
29. **[useful_refinement][high] License termination vs legal-person termination.** A banking license can be cancelled before corporate deregistration; conversely a merger approval can predetermine dissolution before formal registration cancellation.
30. **[useful_refinement][high] Successor institution is an independent field.** A successor may take deposits, selected liabilities or whole business while the predecessor remains in liquidation.
31. **[new_category][high] Residual-estate state.** Resolution can create a good-bank/successor operating business plus a residual estate holding excluded liabilities or recovery claims; do not force every claim into the successor.
32. **[useful_refinement][high] Shareholder wipeout is not depositor loss.** Equity elimination or dilution can coexist with uninterrupted depositor access.
33. **[useful_refinement][high] Institutional-creditor loss is not deposit-insurance payout.** Interbank/bond creditor loss can occur without a retail insured-deposit trigger.
34. **[new_category] Branch-license exit is lower-level than bank exit.** Large numbers of branch closures or permit cancellations should never be counted as bank failures.

## C. False-positive rejection set

35. **[duplicate][high] Guangdong International Trust and Investment Corporation / 广东国际信托投资公司 (GITIC).** Remembered late-1990s bankruptcy is a major financial-institution failure but not a commercial-bank failure; keep it outside the licensed-bank terminal set.
36. **[new_category] Other trust-and-investment-company failures.** Hainan/Guangdong/local trust-and-investment-company closures can resemble bank resolution in old commentary but belong to a different legal perimeter unless a banking license is independently established.
37. **[useful_refinement] Securities-company rescues/closures.** Brokerage takeovers, administrative liquidation or client-asset protection are not bank-resolution events.
38. **[useful_refinement] P2P/platform collapses.** Ezubao-style or later internet-finance failures are borrower/investor fraud or non-bank failures, not insured-deposit bank failures.
39. **[useful_refinement] Wealth-management product losses.** WMP/nonstandard-asset defaults can affect bank customers without implying the issuing/distributing bank is insolvent or exiting.
40. **[useful_refinement] Developer bankruptcy.** Property-company restructurings or liquidation affect bank asset quality but are borrower-resolution events, not bank resolution.
41. **[useful_refinement] LGFV restructuring.** Maturity extension, debt swap or platform consolidation is borrower/public-debt management, not bank legal exit.
42. **[duplicate] Large-bank recapitalization and AMC NPL transfer.** Late-1990s/2000s state-bank recapitalization, bad-asset transfer and corporatization are major repair actions but not terminal bank exits.
43. **[useful_refinement] Listing/delisting.** IPO, privatization or stock-exchange delisting changes securities/ownership status, not banking-license status.
44. **[useful_refinement] Voluntary strategic merger.** Acquisition/absorption of a solvent bank can terminate a legal entity without distress or claimant loss.
45. **[useful_refinement] Village-bank `村改支`.** Independent village-bank legal-person exit through conversion to a successor branch should be treated as policy consolidation unless evidence separately establishes distress and loss allocation.
46. **[new_category] Name change/rebranding.** A renamed bank or converted city cooperative bank is not an exit even if the old institution name disappears from databases.

## D. Claimant-treatment and negative-space tests

47. **[new_category][high] Retail depositor continuity test.** For every candidate terminal case ask whether deposits remained continuously withdrawable at a successor, were frozen then transferred, or were actually reimbursed under deposit insurance.
48. **[new_category][high] Deposit-insurance trigger test.** Deposit-insurance membership, fund participation in resolution and statutory insured-deposit reimbursement must be separately evidenced.
49. **[new_category][high] Uninsured-deposit test.** If deposits exceed the statutory coverage ceiling, actual treatment above the ceiling is needed before labeling the case a depositor haircut or full guarantee.
50. **[new_category][high] Interbank-creditor test.** Baoshang-like cases show that wholesale creditors may be the loss-bearing margin even when retail deposits are stabilized; this is a different claimant channel from depositor insurance.
51. **[new_category][high] Bond/subordinated-debt test.** Capital instruments and subordinated debt can absorb losses before ordinary deposits; exact instrument treatment should later be verified case by case.
52. **[new_category][high] Shareholder-treatment test.** Recapitalization, forced ownership transfer, dilution or equity cancellation must be recorded independently from creditor treatment.
53. **[useful_refinement][high] Successor-contract evidence target.** Later Evidence should seek account-transfer notices, assumption agreements, regulator approvals and license cancellation notices rather than infer continuity from media shorthand such as `接管` or `重组`.
54. **[new_category][high] Negative result: routine court-bankruptcy remains absent.** Blind recall does not recover a broad population of PRC commercial-bank court bankruptcies with transparent depositor waterfalls; true terminal examples remain exceptional against a much larger universe of recapitalization, takeover and merger-based resolution.
