## A. Minimum state machine for modern small-bank reform

1. **[duplicate][high] Policy consolidation is not automatically distress resolution.** A bank can be merged or converted because of province-wide reform even when the immediate legal act is not a takeover, bankruptcy or insured-depositor payout.
2. **[useful_refinement][high] Owner-capital state.** Public capital injection, special-bond-funded equity, state financial-holding investment or sponsor-bank capital support changes ownership/capital before it changes the banking license.
3. **[new_category][high] New-bank formation state.** A province-level rural commercial bank or rural commercial united bank can be newly formed while predecessor institutions continue temporarily; formation date and predecessor exit dates are independent.
4. **[useful_refinement][high] Asset-liability transfer state.** Assets and liabilities can be transferred by agreement/approval without immediate dissolution of every predecessor entity.
5. **[useful_refinement][high] Absorption-merger state.** `吸收合并` implies a successor surviving entity, but exact effective date, scope and whether every asset/liability is assumed remain case facts.
6. **[new_category][high] Branch-conversion state.** `村改支` removes an independent village-bank legal person/license while continuing operations through a branch of a successor bank.
7. **[useful_refinement][high] Operating-state continuity.** Physical outlets, accounts and payment services can continue through a legal transformation; service continuity is not proof the predecessor legal entity survives.
8. **[new_category][high] License-state split.** Banking-license cancellation, branch-license issuance and legal-person dissolution are distinct regulatory events and may occur on different dates.
9. **[useful_refinement][high] Legal-dissolution state.** Corporate deregistration/dissolution should not be inferred merely from merger approval or stock-exchange delisting.
10. **[new_category][high] Customer-contract successor state.** Every exit case needs a field for which legal entity becomes the deposit/loan counterparty after transformation.
11. **[useful_refinement][high] Deposit-insurance state.** Continuation of insured deposits at a successor bank is different from a statutory deposit-insurance payout triggered by failure.
12. **[new_category] Claimant-settlement state.** Depositors, bondholders, interbank creditors, shareholders and other claimants can have different treatment; customer continuity cannot be generalized to all creditors.

## C. Village-bank absorption / `村改支` boundary tests

27. **[duplicate][high] `村改支` is an exit route, not a synonym for failure.** Independent village-bank status can end while customer business is moved into a sponsor/successor bank branch.
28. **[useful_refinement][high] Sponsor ownership is not automatic liability assumption.** A sponsoring bank may own a village bank before reform, but legal transfer of deposits/assets still requires the actual merger/transfer arrangement.
29. **[new_category][high] Branch-license successor.** After conversion, the operating outlet may hold a branch license under the successor bank rather than the predecessor's legal-person banking license.
30. **[useful_refinement][high] Deposit continuity without payout.** If deposits simply transfer to the successor bank, there may be no deposit-insurance reimbursement event even though the predecessor license disappears.
31. **[new_category] Loan continuity.** Borrower loan contracts, collateral registrations and servicing rights may transfer alongside deposits; exact novation/assignment mechanics are case-specific.
32. **[useful_refinement] Payment continuity.** Cards, mobile banking, settlement accounts and account numbers may be migrated operationally; customer-visible continuity is distinct from legal counterparty continuity.
33. **[new_category] Announcement chain.** A complete case may require separate approvals/announcements for merger, dissolution, branch opening and license cancellation rather than one resolution notice.
34. **[useful_refinement][high] Voluntary versus forced boundary.** Some village-bank exits may be strategic/sponsor-led consolidation; others may be high-risk disposal. The same legal form (`吸收合并`/`村改支`) does not reveal motivation.
35. **[new_category] No-branch residual possibility.** Some village banks may be liquidated/closed without a one-for-one successor branch; do not infer branch conversion from legal-person exit alone.
36. **[useful_refinement] Deposit-insurance membership vs payment.** A predecessor village bank may participate in deposit insurance even when no insured-loss payment occurs; membership and payout are different variables.
37. **[new_category][high] Claimant-class asymmetry.** Even if retail deposits transfer seamlessly, subordinated debt, shareholder equity or related-party claims could be treated differently; blind memory lacks case-specific proof.
38. **[useful_refinement] Customer communication as evidence lead.** Successor-account notices and service-migration notices would later be high-value Evidence for contract continuity, but they are not visible in this blind phase.
39. **[new_category] Entity-count metric trap.** Falling numbers of village banks can reflect absorption/branch conversion rather than court insolvency; institution-count decline is not a failure rate.
40. **[useful_refinement][high] Risk-resolution statistics trap.** Official counts of `改革化险` institutions may aggregate capital injections, mergers, closures and governance reform; raw counts should not be mapped directly to bankruptcies.

## D. Bank of Jinzhou / listed-bank status boundary

41. **[duplicate][high] Listing status is not banking-license status.** A bank can delist or privatize while remaining an operating licensed bank.
42. **[useful_refinement][high] Earlier distress versus later corporate action.** Bank of Jinzhou's earlier recapitalization/governance stress can precede a later privatization/delisting; the later transaction should not be retro-labeled as the original intervention event.
43. **[new_category] Shareholder-exit state.** Privatization may remove public shareholders/minority holders from the listed-company register while depositors remain ordinary bank customers.
44. **[useful_refinement] Exchange delisting mechanics.** Scheme/offer thresholds, consideration and delisting date are securities/corporate facts and should not be guessed from bank-resolution memory.
45. **[new_category][high] No remembered depositor impairment.** Blind memory does not recall a 2023-2026 Bank of Jinzhou depositor payout, deposit freeze or license revocation associated merely with privatization/delisting.
46. **[useful_refinement] State ownership is not nationalization proof.** A higher public-sector ownership share after restructuring does not by itself prove statutory nationalization or assumption of all liabilities by government.
