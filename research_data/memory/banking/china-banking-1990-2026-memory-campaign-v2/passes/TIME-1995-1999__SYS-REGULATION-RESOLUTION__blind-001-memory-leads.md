## Pass-level counterexamples and cautions

- Statutory takeover/bankruptcy powers did not imply frequent market exit; major-bank cleanup remained state-led and administrative.
- A statutory 8% capital ratio did not mean banks had already recognized all economic losses.
- City/cooperative consolidation could concentrate weak assets rather than eliminate them.
