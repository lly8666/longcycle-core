## Orthogonal memory leads

1. **Monetary aggregates should be reconstructed from a consolidated monetary/depository-corporation view, not by summing bank product labels.** Period-native `货币当局资产负债表`, `存款性公司概览` or related survey concepts can explain why claims inside the banking system disappear on consolidation while liabilities to money-holding sectors remain.  
   Classification: `new_category` · Importance: high · Confidence: medium-high.

2. **Holder sector and instrument type are separate axes.** A deposit-like instrument held by another bank, a non-bank financial institution, a corporation, household or government can receive different monetary treatment even when the issuing bank calls all of them “deposits” or “certificates”.  
   Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

3. **Old-M1 versus revised-M1 is fundamentally a holder-perimeter break.** The key research question is not merely “which formula changed” but which transaction-capable balances belonged to the money-holding universe at each release vintage.  
   Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

4. **Counterexample: a household cash withdrawal can change old M1 mechanically.** If household demand deposits are outside an older M1 perimeter but M0 cash is inside, moving the same household purchasing power from a demand account into cash could raise measured M1 without creating new transaction liquidity. This is a powerful test of the old definition's signal.  
   Classification: `new_category` · Importance: high · Confidence: medium-high; verify exact historical perimeter before using as fact.

5. **The reverse counterexample differs by definition vintage.** Moving household money from a time deposit into a household demand deposit may leave an older enterprise-focused M1 almost unchanged while raising a revised M1 that includes household demand deposits. Economic behavior is similar; measured narrow money response differs.  
   Classification: `new_category` · Importance: high · Confidence: medium-high.

6. **Corporate demand-to-time migration is a cleaner M1→quasi-money channel.** A corporation shifting current balances into term deposits can reduce narrow-money activity while broad money remains, but the magnitude depends on the period's actual unit-deposit categories.  
   Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

7. **Payment-account migration creates a three-balance-sheet PIT problem.** Cash, commercial-bank household demand deposits and payment-institution customer balances can all support transactions but sit on different balance sheets; a recent M1 redesign appears intended to reduce this measurement mismatch.  
   Classification: `new_category` · Importance: high · Confidence: medium-high.

8. **A definition revision needs at least two temporal fields: effective statistical definition and publication/backcast vintage.** Even if a statistical authority later publishes a revised history, a point-in-time replay before the revision must retain the old release and its then-valid interpretation.  
   Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

9. **Government-bond issuance can create a broad-money/fiscal-deposit wedge before spending.** When households or firms pay for government bonds, balances can move from non-government money holders toward government/fiscal deposits; if the government sector is outside broad money, M2 can behave differently from gross bond financing. Exact settlement mechanics and perimeter must be verified later.  
   Classification: `new_category` · Importance: high · Confidence: medium.

10. **Subsequent fiscal spending can reverse that wedge.** Government expenditure moves balances back toward household/corporate deposits and also changes reserve conditions, so government-bond-heavy TSF and M2 can diverge in timing even without a change in ultimate fiscal impulse.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high at mechanism level.

11. **Interbank consolidation is the reason “同业负债增长” cannot be read as broad-money creation.** An NCD/repo/interbank-deposit expansion may strongly change bank funding and liquidity while netting out of consolidated money-holder liabilities.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

12. **`非银存款` is not synonymous with `同业存款`.** Securities/fund/insurance/trust-sector deposits may belong to a different institutional sector than deposit-taking-bank claims, and different statistical vintages may have changed their M2 treatment. This is a later Evidence priority.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: medium.

13. **Retail/corporate large-denomination certificates and interbank NCDs must not be collapsed.** `大额存单` sold to customer money holders behaves more like a deposit product, while `同业存单` is wholesale financial-institution funding; identical English shorthand “CD” can destroy the money-perimeter distinction.  
    Classification: `new_category` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

14. **WMP is a counterexample to “liquid household wealth = M2”.** A household can move from a bank deposit into a wealth-management claim with little change in perceived liquid wealth while measured deposit-based money changes; whether the WMP invests back into deposits does not make the household claim itself a deposit.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

15. **Money-market funds test functional liquidity versus institutional form.** Highly liquid fund shares can behave transaction-adjacent without being ordinary deposits; memory suggests statistical treatment of some MMF/non-bank balances was later refined. Exact Chinese M2 inclusion mechanics are uncertain and should remain an Evidence lead.  
    Classification: `new_category` · Importance: medium-high · Confidence: low-medium for the revision detail, high for the comparability question.

16. **Securities customer margin/settlement balances are another functional-liquidity test.** Market participation can create transaction-ready balances at securities institutions that old bank-deposit categories may miss, motivating later statistical perimeter changes. Exact historical component treatment is uncertain.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: medium-high · Confidence: low-medium.

17. **M2 versus TSF remains deliberately non-identical.** Money-holder liabilities and financing-to-real-economy instruments differ in sector, instrument and stock/flow boundaries.  
    Classification: `duplicate` · Importance: high · Confidence: high. Kept only as an anti-drift guard.

18. **M0 versus reserve/base money remains deliberately non-identical.** Currency circulation and the central-bank reserve base answer different questions.  
    Classification: `duplicate` · Importance: high · Confidence: high. Kept only as an anti-drift guard.

19. **A reserve-requirement cut does not mechanically create the same amount of M2.** It changes banks' required reserve constraint and potentially money-multiplier conditions; actual broad-money creation still depends on lending, asset purchases, borrower demand and deposit/fiscal flows.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

20. **FX settlement can connect reserve money and broad money without making them identical.** Conversion of foreign-currency receipts into RMB deposits can alter commercial-bank assets/liabilities and, depending on central-bank intervention, reserve money; holder sector and sterilization determine the measured path.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: medium-high · Confidence: high at mechanism level.

21. **Loan write-off/repayment and deposit destruction need separate accounting.** A decline in loan stock, a write-off, a cash repayment and a transfer from one depositor to another have different effects on bank assets and money-holder liabilities; loan growth cannot be used as a mechanical M2 bridge.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: medium-high · Confidence: high.

22. **M2/GDP should be decomposed before judging monetary excess.** Financial deepening, high deposit preference, bank-centric intermediation, asset monetization and nominal-GDP cycles can all move the ratio; a cross-decade rise is not by itself proof of progressively easier policy.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

23. **Definition changes can create a level break without the same growth-rate break—or vice versa.** Whether headline year-on-year growth remains comparable depends on whether both current and base-period observations are restated to the same perimeter. A published growth rate and a simple growth rate calculated from mixed-vintage levels are not guaranteed equivalent.  
    Classification: `new_category` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

24. **Backcast depth is a PIT property.** A new definition may be reconstructed only for a limited historical window, while earlier observations remain under older definitions or require bridging. Longcycle should not infer an all-history homogeneous series merely because today's database shows a long column with one metric name.  
    Classification: `useful_refinement` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.

25. **Component contribution can be more stable than headline interpretation only if component taxonomy is also versioned.** A weak M1 can reflect household/enterprise deposit mix, termization, payment migration, statistical reclassification or actual activity weakness; storing component shares without their own definition vintage merely moves the ambiguity down one level.  
    Classification: `new_category` · Importance: high · Confidence: high.
