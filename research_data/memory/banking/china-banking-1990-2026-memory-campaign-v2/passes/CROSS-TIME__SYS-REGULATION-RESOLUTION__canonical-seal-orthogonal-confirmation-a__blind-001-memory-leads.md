## Bounded independent scan

This pass intentionally does not retell chronology or enumerate known cases. It asks only whether an independent memory scan of terminal/near-terminal states, claimant/customer continuity, negative space, period-native state vocabulary and counterexamples changes the compact category map.

1. **useful_refinement — source-native state label and normalized state should be separate fields.** Recalled Chinese labels such as `接管`, `停业整顿`, `撤销`, `关闭`, `解散`, `破产` or merger/absorption language should be preserved as period/source-native wording first; a normalized analytical state should be assigned separately. Similar-looking labels should not be treated as legal synonyms from memory alone. Importance: high. This changes representation discipline, not the top-level ontology.

2. **useful_refinement — customer continuity has at least an operational and a legal-successor dimension.** Continued access to deposits, accounts, branches/cards/payment rails or service channels is not by itself proof of which legal entity ultimately bears a customer's claim; conversely a legal successor can exist even when operations are temporarily disrupted. Importance: medium. This refines the existing claimant/customer-continuity category rather than adding a new category.

3. **duplicate — terminal legal-entity state is distinct from branch/network closure.** A branch closure, outlet consolidation, channel shutdown or business-line exit does not establish entity-level terminal resolution.

4. **duplicate — administrative intervention is not automatically terminal.** Takeover, supervisory custody, rectification or other strong intervention labels may be near-terminal or reversible; terminal status requires an additional entity-state transition.

5. **duplicate — merger/absorption is a consolidation counterexample unless failure/resolution facts independently support a failure classification.** Voluntary or policy-driven consolidation cannot be converted into failure merely because the predecessor legal entity disappears.

6. **duplicate — claimant protection source is separate from claimant outcome.** A depositor may retain access or receive transferred claims without that fact identifying whether support came from deposit insurance, a successor institution, public capital, liquidity support or another mechanism.

7. **duplicate — payment timing and legal resolution timing can differ.** Customer access may be restored before final legal cleanup, while liquidation/recovery can continue after operational continuity has moved elsewhere.

8. **duplicate — deposit transfer is not identical to statutory insurance payout.** An economic outcome that protects depositors should not be back-labeled as an insurance payout without period-native evidence.

9. **duplicate — claimant classes remain heterogeneous.** Household deposits, corporate deposits, interbank claims, senior wholesale liabilities, subordinated/capital instruments and equity should not be collapsed into a single `creditor loss` state.

10. **duplicate — residual entity and successor entity can coexist during cleanup.** Customer-facing continuity may move to a successor while impaired assets, litigation, recoveries or residual liabilities remain in another legal shell/estate/vehicle.

11. **duplicate — old-state vocabulary is time-sensitive.** A post-2015 deposit-insurance or post-2020s resolution-planning vocabulary should not be retroactively imposed on 1990s/early-2000s events when contemporaneous terminology may have been different.

12. **duplicate — near-terminal distress is not the same as terminal exit.** Severe liquidity stress, recapitalization, NPL disposal, AMC transfer, management replacement or local-government rescue can occur without legal-entity termination.

13. **duplicate — customer service continuity is not evidence of zero economic loss elsewhere.** Maintaining depositor access may coexist with losses to shareholders, capital instruments, public funds, asset sellers or other claimants.

14. **duplicate — bad-asset transfer and liability succession remain separable.** The party that receives impaired assets need not be the party that assumes deposits or other customer claims.

15. **duplicate — `closed`, `revoked`, `dissolved`, `bankrupt`, `merged` and `taken over` require minimum-fact gates before normalized classification.** Memory of a strong word alone is not sufficient to infer exact legal procedure, authority, date or creditor treatment.

16. **duplicate — false-positive negative space remains material.** Trust/securities failures, P2P collapse, WMP losses, developer bankruptcy, LGFV restructuring, recapitalization, delisting, branch closures and routine rural-bank conversion/consolidation should not be counted as commercial-bank terminal resolution without entity-level facts.
