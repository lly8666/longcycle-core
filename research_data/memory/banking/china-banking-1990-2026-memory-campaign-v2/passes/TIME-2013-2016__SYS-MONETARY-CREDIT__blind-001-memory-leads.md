## Broad recall leads

### 1. The 2013 interbank liquidity shock (“钱荒”) is a regime-defining money-market event
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Memory strongly associates mid-2013 with extraordinary spikes in interbank money-market rates and severe short-term liquidity stress.
- The event matters not merely as a rate print but as a test of bank reliance on wholesale funding, maturity transformation and expectations about central-bank accommodation.
- Exact dates and peak rates are Evidence work.

### 2. The initial policy response appears to have included a deliberate liquidity-discipline signal before stabilization
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- A key contemporaneous interpretation was that the central bank was not immediately validating every institution’s liquidity demand, partly to discourage excessive leverage or poor liquidity management.
- This should be separated from a claim that authorities intended a general macro recessionary tightening.
- Later liquidity support can coexist with the earlier discipline signal within the same event window.

### 3. Standing Lending Facility (SLF) introduced a more explicit standing-liquidity backstop layer
- **Novelty:** new_category
- `常备借贷便利` is remembered as an evolving central-bank facility for short-term liquidity support to eligible institutions, with a rate potentially useful as an upper-bound/corridor reference.
- It is not the same object as OMO repo/reverse-repo transactions or an unsecured interbank rate.
- Exact eligible institutions, collateral and maturity terms require verification.

### 4. Short-term liquidity operations / ad-hoc tools formed an experimental bridge around the new framework
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Memory includes `短期流动性调节工具`/SLO-style operations as part of the period’s experimentation with more flexible liquidity management.
- Whether and how long this instrument remained operational is uncertain; preserve it as a dead-end/evolution lead rather than a durable policy-rate assumption.

### 5. Medium-term Lending Facility (MLF) added a term-liquidity and price signal beyond overnight/short OMO
- **Novelty:** new_category
- `中期借贷便利` is remembered as supplying medium-term central-bank funding against collateral to selected banks.
- It helped create a richer term structure of central-bank liquidity operations and later gained importance for rate transmission.
- Do not backcast its later centrality into its earliest years.

### 6. Pledged Supplementary Lending (PSL) linked central-bank funding to policy/development-bank transmission
- **Novelty:** new_category
- `抵押补充贷款` is remembered as longer-horizon targeted liquidity/funding associated especially with policy/development-bank lending and selected policy projects.
- Its transmission differs from reserve cuts or broad OMO because the institutional channel and use of funds are narrower.
- Exact counterparties, collateral and program linkages require Evidence.

### 7. The first-generation Loan Prime Rate (LPR) was a quoted lending reference, not the post-2019 LPR mechanism
- **Novelty:** new_category
- An LPR framework emerged in this period using quotations from banks to create a market-oriented reference for lending pricing.
- It coexisted with benchmark-rate legacy and did not yet have the later explicit linkage architecture familiar after the 2019 reform.
- Historical data must version the two regimes separately even though both use the name `LPR/贷款基础利率`.

### 8. Lending-rate liberalization and deposit-rate liberalization did not occur as one simultaneous switch
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Memory is that restrictions on bank loan pricing were relaxed earlier/more quickly, while the deposit-rate ceiling remained a more binding constraint for longer and was widened/removed later.
- Asset pricing and liability pricing therefore liberalized on different timetables.
- Exact sequencing/bands are Evidence work.

### 9. Removal of the deposit-rate ceiling changed funding competition as well as monetary transmission
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Once banks had substantially greater deposit-pricing freedom, deposit franchise, customer stickiness and competition for liabilities mattered more directly for funding cost.
- This complicates any simple mapping from a benchmark-rate cut to the same deposit-cost change at every bank.

### 10. “定向降准” became a distinct targeted-reserve policy vocabulary
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Targeted reserve-ratio reductions are remembered as being used to support particular institution classes or lending priorities such as `三农`/small and micro businesses.
- A targeted RRR cut differs from a uniform system-wide reserve reduction and should be represented with eligibility scope.
- Exact qualification rules and dates require Evidence.

### 11. 2014–2015 shifted from targeted support toward repeated broad easing as growth/disinflation pressures increased
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Memory associates the later part of the slice with multiple benchmark-rate cuts and required-reserve reductions, alongside ongoing targeted tools.
- The period therefore contains both “targeted easing” and “broad easing”; one label should not erase instrument mix or timing.

### 12. FX flows changed from a structural source of RMB liquidity toward a potential drain
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Earlier reliance on FX purchases as a base-money source weakened as capital flows and currency expectations changed; by the later period, FX reserve decline/outflows could withdraw domestic liquidity.
- This altered the central bank’s balance-sheet problem from sterilizing persistent inflows toward supplying domestic liquidity through RRR cuts and facilities.
- Exact flow timing and magnitudes remain uncertain.

### 13. The 2015 RMB exchange-rate/fixing episode created a monetary-liquidity feedback channel
- **Novelty:** new_category
- A change in currency-fixing/market expectations is remembered as contributing to depreciation pressure and capital outflow dynamics.
- FX intervention/reserve changes could then affect domestic base money, requiring offsetting liquidity management.
- This is a cross-border-liquidity mechanism lead, not an exact claim about one day’s fixing move.

### 14. An interest-rate-corridor style framework was emerging before a single modern policy rate existed
- **Novelty:** new_category
- The combination of standing-facility rates, OMO rates, excess-reserve remuneration and money-market rates increasingly supported corridor-style thinking.
- But the system still had multiple administered and market prices; treating one rate as the sole policy rate would be anachronistic.

### 15. MPA around 2016 looks like a successor/evolution from earlier differentiated-reserve macroprudential controls
- **Novelty:** new_category
- `宏观审慎评估体系 / MPA` is remembered as broadening bank-specific assessment beyond a simple loan-growth or reserve-ratio lever, incorporating wider credit/macroeconomic prudential dimensions.
- It should be distinguished both from ordinary microprudential supervision and from the earlier `差别准备金动态调整` mechanism.
- Exact indicators/weights/formulas are source-detail work.

### 16. Social Financing remained essential because shadow/non-loan channels changed the effective credit pulse
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Trust loans, entrusted loans, acceptances, bonds and other components could accelerate or contract differently from RMB bank loans.
- Monetary transmission therefore increasingly required watching both bank balance-sheet credit and broader financing composition.

### 17. 2013 money-market stress exposed wholesale-funding and maturity-mismatch heterogeneity across banks
- **Novelty:** useful_refinement
- Joint-stock banks and some smaller institutions may have been more sensitive than deposit-rich large banks to interbank/repo funding conditions.
- The same reserve position or aggregate liquidity condition thus had unequal effects across bank types.

### 18. Local-government debt-swap/refinancing developments could alter bank credit creation without being ordinary new macro credit
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Around the mid-2010s, local-government debt restructuring/swap mechanisms are remembered as moving exposures between loans and bonds or changing refinancing structure.
- That can affect loan growth, bond holdings, duration and liquidity without representing the same thing as fresh project-credit demand.
- Exact program/legal details belong to Evidence and other shards.

### 19. PSL/policy-bank lending created a targeted long-duration transmission channel distinct from commercial-bank loan demand
- **Novelty:** new_category
- Policy-bank funding linked to housing/infrastructure or other policy priorities could support fixed investment even when commercial-bank risk appetite was uneven.
- The mechanism combines central-bank liquidity with policy-bank balance sheets; do not collapse it into generic “credit easing.”

### 20. Deposit competition and wealth-management alternatives weakened the old administered-deposit-rate transmission model
- **Novelty:** new_category
- As deposit pricing liberalized and household alternatives expanded, banks increasingly faced liability competition and possible migration from conventional deposits.
- Funding-cost transmission could therefore diverge across banks and across deposit/customer segments.

### 21. “稳健 / 松紧适度” style stance labels should not be treated as one-dimensional instrument settings
- **Novelty:** duplicate
- Period communication could retain a formally “prudent” stance while using targeted and later broad easing tools.
- This reiterates the existing lesson that contemporaneous stance labels, actual liquidity operations and borrower-specific credit conditions must remain separate objects.

## Negative space / counterexamples

- `钱荒` is not proof that aggregate monetary policy was uniformly tight for the entire 2013–2016 window.
- SLF, MLF, PSL, OMO reverse repo, RRR cuts and relending are not interchangeable liquidity instruments; counterparties, maturity, collateral, targeting and price signals differ.
- The pre-2019 LPR must not be silently overwritten by the post-2019 LPR mechanism.
- Loan-rate liberalization and deposit-rate liberalization are separate transitions.
- A fall in FX-purchase liquidity can make domestic liquidity injection necessary without a change in the stated macro stance.
- MPA is not merely a renamed RRR and should not be projected backward before its own adoption/vintage.
- Targeted easing can coexist with tight conditions for other banks or borrowers.
- Local-government debt swaps/refinancing can alter credit/bond statistics without the same meaning as new lending.
- No material pre-1990 antecedent emerged.
