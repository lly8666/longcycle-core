2. A bank can gain market share because it has better underwriting, because peers are capital constrained, or because it is more willing to fund asset growth through wholesale channels; the observed growth rate does not identify the mechanism. [new_category]
3. Young 2009-2010 vintages mean contemporaneous profitability and NPL ratios are especially poor through-cycle indicators. [useful_refinement][high]
