## Orthogonal challenge leads

1. **Policy-rate signal, liquidity instrument and loan-pricing benchmark are three different layers.** Seven-day OMO rate can be the short policy signal; MLF can remain a term-liquidity instrument; LPR is a bank quotation/loan-pricing reference. Treating all three as one “benchmark rate” loses the operating framework. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

2. **MLF’s role may have shifted not only in rate signaling but also in sequencing and quantity provision.** Memory suggests late-period reforms weakened the old habit of reading every MLF operation as the immediate LPR anchor. Exact tender mechanism and sequence need Evidence. `new_category`, high importance, medium confidence.

3. **DR007 is a market outcome, not the policy rate itself.** The informative object is the spread/distribution of secured interbank repo rates around the policy signal, including collateral, counterparty and calendar effects. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

4. **Temporary repo/reverse-repo tools are best interpreted as corridor or deviation-management candidates, not routine stance changes.** Their existence may reveal the intended upper/lower control logic around the short policy rate. Exact corridor construction is deferred. `new_category`, medium-high importance, medium confidence.

5. **`买断式逆回购` should be separated from pledged reverse repo and MLF on legal/economic mechanics.** The ownership/collateral treatment and maturity profile may make it a medium-term liquidity-management instrument with different balance-sheet presentation. Exact contract mechanics require source work. `new_category`, high importance, medium confidence.

6. **Central-bank government-bond purchases and sales can be two-sided liquidity operations.** A purchase may inject reserves and a sale may drain; the existence of transactions does not by itself imply a permanent balance-sheet expansion program. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

7. **Government-bond trading also has a yield-curve / market-expectation dimension.** Memory suggests the central bank paid attention to very low long-bond yields and duration/interest-rate risk, so bond operations or communication may combine monetary implementation with market-risk signaling rather than pure easing. `new_category`, high importance, medium-high confidence.

8. **Fiscal-deposit timing can reverse the apparent liquidity effect of bond issuance.** Bond settlement can move funds from bank reserves/deposits into Treasury fiscal deposits, while later fiscal spending releases them back. OMO/RRR actions may smooth this path. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

9. **RRR impact is heterogeneous across bank categories and reserve bases.** A headline cut does not imply the same released liquidity, funding substitution or lending response for every bank. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

10. **“RRR cut + MLF maturity” should be netted before inferring durable liquidity.** Replacement of expensive/term central-bank funding with reserve release can change funding cost and duration even if net reserves rise much less than the gross RRR number. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

11. **Deposit-pricing discipline includes more than posted benchmark reductions.** A late-period regulatory/self-discipline issue likely involved banks paying extra interest or benefits outside posted deposit rates—period-native vocabulary may include `手工补息`—and curbing it could abruptly lower corporate-deposit cost or shift deposits. `new_category`, high importance, medium-high confidence.

12. **Corporate deposit repricing and household term-deposit repricing have different speeds.** Corporate negotiated deposits can react quickly to discipline changes, while household term deposits roll over slowly; average liability cost therefore has multiple betas. `new_category`, high importance, high confidence.

13. **NCD funding can reprice faster than sticky retail term deposits and may transmit policy easing differently across small versus large banks.** Research should track NCD yield, issuance access and deposit substitution separately from average deposit cost. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

14. **NIM relief can lag both loan-rate and deposit-rate policy actions.** Existing assets/liabilities have different repricing clocks, and weak loan demand can compress new-loan spreads even while old deposits remain expensive. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

15. **Existing-mortgage adjustment has at least four semantic objects: benchmark, contractual add-point/margin, eligible borrower/loan perimeter and repricing cycle.** A policy can change one without changing the others. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

16. **Multiple rounds of legacy-mortgage relief should not be collapsed into one event.** Memory suggests an earlier repricing mechanism and later further convergence/negotiation or repricing-cycle changes; exact dates and eligibility require Evidence. `new_category`, high importance, medium confidence.

17. **Household mortgage support, developer/project financing and housing-inventory acquisition are distinct risk channels.** A central-bank relending facility can cheapen funding for an intermediary while the acquiring entity/project and commercial bank still bear separate risks. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

18. **PSL and affordable-housing relending should not be merged.** PSL is associated with policy-bank/pledged funding architecture, while affordable-housing relending appears to support a different chain of bank lending or inventory acquisition. Their fiscal/quasi-fiscal and credit-risk locations may differ. `new_category`, high importance, medium-high confidence.

19. **Local-debt swaps can mechanically reduce bank-loan growth while raising government-bond financing in TSF.** If LGFV/local obligations are repaid or replaced with government bonds, bank assets shift from loans/opaque claims toward bonds or cash even without a fall in total public-sector financing. `new_category`, high importance, high confidence.

20. **Debt replacement changes yield, maturity and capital/risk treatment as well as creditor identity.** A lower-yield government bond replacing a higher-yield local exposure can improve credit quality while pressuring bank asset yield/NIM and increasing duration. `new_category`, high importance, medium-high confidence.

21. **“挤水分” / anti-idling cleanup is a candidate explanation for slower headline money/credit growth that is not equivalent to tighter effective policy.** Removing deposit-loan arbitrage, manual interest subsidies or financial-system round-tripping can reduce M2/loan statistics while improving data quality. `new_category`, high importance, medium confidence.

22. **TSF composition can shift from bank loans toward government bonds, creating a quantity-composition regime change.** Comparing total growth across vintages without component shares can falsely infer unchanged transmission. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

23. **Capital-market support facilities require a separate collateral/liquidity/risk map.** A swap facility for securities/fund/insurance institutions and a relending channel for listed-company/shareholder repurchases are not ordinary corporate loans, not direct equity purchases by the central bank, and not necessarily the same risk bearer. `new_category`, high importance, medium-high confidence.

24. **Structural relending facilities should be compared by ex-ante eligibility, ex-post loan verification, funding percentage/cost and ultimate credit-risk holder.** Technology/equipment, carbon/green and inclusive-small-micro tools may share a family resemblance but not identical mechanics. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

25. **Low CPI/PPI or weak inflation expectations can keep real borrowing costs restrictive despite nominal easing.** The relevant borrower state depends on sector-specific output prices, wage/income growth and debt cash flows, not only headline CPI. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

26. **FX constraint is better modeled as a reaction-function tradeoff than a hard prohibition.** Domestic easing may continue alongside RMB pressure through fixing, macroprudential or liquidity-management tools; the mix and timing may change rather than easing simply stopping. `useful_refinement`, high importance, high confidence.

27. **Duplicate anchor: government-bond-heavy TSF must be decomposed from private credit demand.** This repeats the compact broad coverage and is retained only as an explicit cross-check. `duplicate`, medium importance, high confidence.

## Negative-space and counterexample audit

- A lower short policy rate does not mean MLF, LPR, DR007 and actual loan rates all move one-for-one.
- Central-bank bond transactions can be two-sided and do not establish QE by label alone.
- Fiscal bond issuance can temporarily drain reserves before spending returns liquidity.
- Headline RRR release can overstate net reserve addition and can have heterogeneous bank effects.
- Lower posted deposit rates do not equal immediate average funding-cost relief; discipline against off-table interest can matter separately.
- A fall in bank-loan growth can partly reflect local-debt replacement into government bonds or anti-idling cleanup, not only weak/new credit supply.
- Existing-mortgage repricing, new-mortgage floors and developer financing are different objects.
- Structural tools and capital-market support facilities do not imply the central bank bears final borrower/equity risk.
- Low nominal rates do not necessarily mean low real rates for stressed sectors.
