# ADC blind-recall plan v1

This plan was fixed before ADC historical search/self-verification.

## Scope

Antibody-drug conjugates (ADC), China-linked global development and business development, with enough global landmark context to make China-linked trajectories interpretable.

## Pass family

1. Platform/mechanism exhaustion.
2. Asset–indication–trial/regimen/readout exhaustion.
3. Rights/partner/ownership and China-global BD exhaustion.
4. Regulatory/commercial outcome exhaustion.
5. Failure/revision/counterexample exhaustion.
6. Atlas-only negative-space passes until the saturation gate is satisfied.

## Hard blind instruction

Use only unsourced model memory and the already-generated blind atlas. Do not browse, search, retrieve sources, inspect ADC source documents, or consume search snippets before seal. Do not turn recollection into Evidence. Preserve uncertainty instead of manufacturing dates, numbers or exact deal/trial terms.

## Researcher-facing pressure

The sealed atlas should make it possible to choose a small adversarial set that jointly stresses:

- molecule versus indication versus trial/regimen identity;
- endpoint/readout maturity and safety;
- molecule ownership versus regional development/commercial rights;
- regulatory state versus commercial outcome;
- success, failure, withdrawal, revision and rescue;
- China-origin assets with global partners and jurisdictionally split states.
